### Agenda item: Motorola public safety radio purchase
What happened: James Cornwell-Shiel presented a fully grant-funded recommendation to buy Motorola public safety radios for the Laurel Police Department. He said the purchase would complete the upgrade of officer-worn radios with 53 Motorola N70 radios (the transcript records "50 53") and accessories, replacing radios largely bought before 2013, and include a pilot group of eight APX 8500 mobile radios for police vehicles, city emergency vehicles, and two dispatch stations. He said funding comes from the Maryland State Aid for Police Protection Communications Encryption Program grant, applied for in August, and that pricing uses an existing Prince George's County contract with Motorola Solutions. He recommended approval in the amount of $499,990 to Motorola Solutions Incorporated of Chicago, Illinois. He said the grant is contingent on project close-out by June 2027.

In response to Councilmember Simmons, he said the radios generally last at least 10 years, and that current radios still function but are end of life with no replacement parts available; 23 of 80 portables were replaced in 2023.

Resident relevance: Not established beyond police and emergency communications equipment.

Outcome / next step: President Smith said the item would go to a meeting later in November, after the budget amendment passes, not to that evening's meeting.
