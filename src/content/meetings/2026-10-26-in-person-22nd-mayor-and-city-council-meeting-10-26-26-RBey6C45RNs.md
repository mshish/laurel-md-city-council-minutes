## Discussion by agenda item

### Agenda item: Charter Amendment Resolution No. 183 budget adoption date
What happened: President Smith introduced the second public hearing with possible action on Charter Amendment Resolution No. 183, amending Article 500, Section 503 of the City Charter to change the date by which the Council passes the annual general operating and capital improvement program budget from June 30 to June 20. Director Saylor said the Maryland General Assembly in 2025 amended Tax-Property Article 6-303 to require municipal governing bodies to set the property tax rate before June 20; she said the state law does not require a charter change, but keeping the charter in line with state law is better practice. With no one signed up, President Smith opened and closed the public hearing at 6:39 p.m.

Councilman Simmons proposed a written amendment that would keep the June 30 budget deadline and add a sentence requiring the Council to set property tax rates by June 20, to avoid conflating the state requirement with budget passage. He described three options: no change, which he called legally valid; setting the tax-rate date, which he favored; or moving the budget date, which he said would remove flexibility and be "harmful to the city's budget process." City Solicitor Anderson said the Simmons proposal was not legally insufficient but recommended aligning the budget date with state law so the charter itself guides the process. Councilman Kole said tax rates are set through the budget under Section 514, so the proposed language did not belong in Section 503, and supported moving the budget deadline to June 20. President Smith said he agreed more with moving the date. Councilman Mills supported combining the two approaches.

A speaker not identified in the transcript said council rules do not allow a verbal motion to amend legislation and that it must be in writing [00:47:38]. Another unidentified speaker said charter amendments must address one topic at a time under state law [00:50:51]. Director Saylor said the budget ordinance includes the tax rate and asked how a separate tax-rate ordinance would work [00:55:18]. A speaker said a separate tax-rate ordinance would need to be ready by June 13 to allow advertising, and noted ordinances require two public hearings [00:59:52]. Councilman Simmons said the City typically adopts the budget by the end of May.

Resident relevance: The resolution concerns when the Council must adopt the budget and property tax rate each year.

Outcome / next step: Both the amendment and the resolution received two yes and two no votes; no result was announced for either. Result not stated in transcript.
