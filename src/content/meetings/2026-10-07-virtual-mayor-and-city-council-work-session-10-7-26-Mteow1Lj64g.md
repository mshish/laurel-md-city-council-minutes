### Agenda item: Charter Resolution No. 183 budget adoption date
What happened: The presiding officer introduced Charter Amendment/Charter Resolution No. 183, amending Article 500 (Finance), Section 503 of the City Charter to change the date by which the council must pass the annual general operating and capital improvement program budget from June 30 to June 20. Director Saylor said that in 2025 the Maryland General Assembly amended Property Tax Article 6-303 to require municipalities to set the next year's tax rate before June 20, and that the city sets its tax rate through the budget ordinance.

Councilmember Simmons asked whether the tax rate could be set separately from the budget. Director Saylor said it could be, but it fits within the budget process because property tax is the largest revenue source. Councilmember Simmons, citing property tax as roughly 65 to 69% of revenue, suggested instead requiring only the tax rate by June 20 if a budget is not passed, keeping up to 10 more days in extraordinary cases.

Councilmember Clark asked whether the change shortens the council's review time and how it affects the newspaper notice window. Director Saylor confirmed the deadline moves from June 30 to June 20, and that if the council fails to adopt, the mayor's proposed budget takes effect by default. The newspaper notice question was not directly answered. Mayor Sydnor said the review period goes from 60 to 51 days after the budget arrives May 1, and that members can contact department directors and discuss the budget before hearings. Councilmember Clark said review time has been a concern for a couple of years and that cutting 10 days without adding time at the front may cause issues.

Councilmember Simmons said draft legislation on the number of days the council sees the budget was expected in November. He said that in his opinion no charter amendment is legally required, that the city set its tax rate before June 20 this year, and that repeating the state date in the charter could cause complications if the state changes it. He said he did not oppose moving it forward for first hearing.

Resident relevance: If adopted, the budget would be adopted 10 days earlier, which may shorten the time for council review and public input.

Outcome / next step: Moved to the October 14, 2026 meeting for first hearing.
